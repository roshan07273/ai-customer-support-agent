# Northstar Commerce Refund Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-01

Agents must validate refund requests against this policy before approving, denying, or escalating.

## Standard Eligibility

1. Physical products are refundable within 30 days of delivery.
2. The order must be delivered or fulfilled before a refund can be processed.
3. The order payment status must be `paid`.
4. The order must not already have an approved, pending, or completed refund.
5. The customer must provide a clear reason for the refund.

## Non-Refundable Rules

1. Final sale items are not refundable.
2. Opened hygiene or personal care products are not refundable unless the reason is confirmed defective or damaged.
3. Digital products are not refundable after access has been used.
4. Items with missing required serial numbers are not auto-refundable.
5. Orders with an open chargeback cannot be refunded by the agent.

## Risk And Escalation

1. Refund amounts above $300 require human approval.
2. Customers with a risk score of 60 or higher require human review.
3. Customers with 3 or more refunds in the last 180 days require human review.
4. Hazmat items can be approved only when a compliant return workflow is available; otherwise escalate.
5. Oversized items may be refundable, but the customer must be told pickup coordination is required.

## Agent Tone

The agent must be friendly, concise, and firm. If a request violates the policy, the agent should explain the exact rule and offer the next best option, such as warranty support, exchange review, or human escalation.
